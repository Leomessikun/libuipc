Date: 2026-09-12. Primary sources only. Research hypotheses below are proposals, not established novelty claims. Search covered robot-assisted dressing, force/contact prediction, tactile feasibility and recovery, emphasizing 2024–2026 with older direct predecessors. This is not an exhaustive systematic review. ## Main finding

The broad idea “predict spatial contact forces under candidate actions, identify trouble, and choose a recovery” is already substantially occupied. Adding diffusion, privileged training labels, or a jam classifier does not establish novelty. The defensible next step is an information-value experiment: determine whether calibrated distributed labels improve recovery action decisions beyond aggregate wrench supervision under identical visual/proprioceptive deployment observations.

## Most recent dressing teleoperation + diffusion candidate

[Dressing in Motion: A Human Motion-Aware Diffusion Policy for Robot-Assisted Dressing](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.04759v1), submitted September 4, 2026, is a strong candidate for the user's reference; identity is not confirmed. It learns from static-arm expert demonstrations and adapts action chunks using observed arm motion. §V reports 180 simulated teleoperation trajectories and 210 real SpaceMouse demonstrations; simulation and real policies use independent datasets and are explicitly not sim-to-real transfer. The real study includes nine participants, three garment types and six motion patterns. The policy is visual/proprioceptive, but hardware uses a tool-force safety threshold: do not describe the entire setup as having no force sensing. This is strong competition for generic “teleoperation + diffusion + adaptation,” while not a distributed-force recovery study.

## Narrow research hypothesis worth testing

**At equal deployment observations, data and model budget, do action-conditioned distributed contact labels enable better recovery-action ranking than action-conditioned aggregate wrench labels in cloth snag states with similar visible geometry, task progress and resultant wrench?**

Potential output is a joint prediction of future regional normal/shear load, probability of unblocking, and progress conditional on candidate action. This is a targeted combination and evaluation question, not a claim that any component is first. The central contribution would have to be demonstrated decision-relevant information beyond existing representations, particularly on unseen garments, friction regimes and occlusions. Merely achieving lower pointwise force MSE is insufficient.

A useful falsifier is finding no advantage after matching baselines on model capacity, amount of failure data, action candidate set and observation history. If a wrench auxiliary head or geometry-only future-progress predictor gives the same action ranking and recovery, abandon the stronger distributed-force claim.

## Minimum decisive experiment

1. Construct snag states with independently verified contact/topology causes, including hand/fist catch, elbow catch, twisted sleeve, and ordinary tension without blockage. Include compound and ambiguous cases rather than forcing mutually exclusive labels. Define labels from geometry/contact and successful interventions, not from the same force threshold the predictor learns.
2. Branch short candidate trajectories from the **same full simulator state**, retaining cloth positions, velocities, material state, body/grasp state and any solver-dependent state required for repeatability. The candidate action must be an actual intervention. Executed-trajectory prediction alone does not demonstrate counterfactual action comparison.
3. Record synchronized progress, grasp validity, load exposure and normal/tangential force fields for each branch. Keep body-region coordinates stable across meshes, preserve spatial resolution explicitly and evaluate sensitivity to discretization.
4. Match state pairs by initial progress, aggregate wrench and visible geometry as far as feasible. Report matching error and evaluate an unmatched natural distribution too; hard matching must not manufacture the entire result. For claimed future label information, compare full future wrench supervision as well as present wrench.
5. Train equal-input models: visual/proprio history only; same plus future geometry/progress auxiliary targets; aggregate force/moment auxiliary targets; regional normal targets; regional normal-plus-shear targets. Same actor observations, candidate actions, data count, failure count and parameter/computation budgets. A shuffled-spatial-label control checks whether improvement comes from a generic auxiliary loss.
6. Include adapted VHR-normal-map planning, Deep-Haptic-MPC-style planning and tactile-guided baselines with sensor access explicitly separated. Oracle current/full future force maps provide a ceiling, not a deployable baseline. A camera-only student must never receive privileged mesh positions, force-derived reward components or labels through its observation path.
7. Primary metrics: recovery completion and eventual dressing completion; recovery-action ranking/regret; local peak load and time-integrated exposure; time and number of failed recovery attempts. Report force reductions jointly with progress so standing still cannot win. Evaluate jam detection at fixed false-positive rates and uncertainty calibration, not only accuracy on balanced synthetic labels.

## Two risks that could invalidate this direction

**Observability.** Training privilege cannot create missing deployment information. Hidden prestress, friction and occluded contact can produce similar observed histories but require different actions. A distribution or abstention/probing mechanism may be warranted. Deliberate probing itself has substantial active-perception precedent; its role must be quantified rather than advertised as novel. Test visual history length and small reversible probes before claiming force-field identifiability.

**Force ground truth.** The parent team's local audit found cached IPC gradients can be stale or omit the friction equilibrium contribution under loose stopping tolerances. Treat that as a dataset-validity blocker for vector-force claims, not evidence that friction is absent. Candidate branches need calibrated stage-aligned force labels, timestep/tolerance metadata, explicit unavailable-versus-zero masks, geometry mapping checks and residual diagnostics. A solver-tolerance artifact cannot be learned into a physically meaningful shear model.

Real validation must distinguish evaluation sensing from actor inputs. An instrumented phantom or independent distributed-load measurement may be used solely for evaluation even if deployment policy is camera/proprio only. Matching wrist wrench alone cannot establish correctness of the spatial distribution. If such validation is unavailable, limit the result to simulation action value and real recovery outcomes, and do not claim verified local human load reduction.

## Reporting boundaries

The bounded search did not establish that this precise controlled comparison already exists, but that is not proof of absence. Cite the six competitors above and state the proposed question directly. Avoid “first force-aware dressing,” “first spatial force prediction,” “first counterfactual force model,” “first jam recovery,” and “first diffusion dressing.” An implementation using IPC is a platform choice unless it enables validated labels or intervention experiments that reveal a previously untested phenomenon.
